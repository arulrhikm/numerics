## Additional curves

- **Trotter p = 6 (analytic, no Richardson)**: closed form C_p^(1+1/p) · ε^(-1/p) · (2/(1+p))^(1/p) with C_6 = 50
- **envelope (right panel)**: Best Trotter taken over p ∈ {1, 2, 4, 6}; Best extrapolated taken over p ∈ {1, 2, 4} and both schedules.
